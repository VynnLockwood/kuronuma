## Decisions and assumptions
- Last month is the reference; no budgets (they need a table and the owner's approval).
- A currency gets a card when it has spending in either window or a recurring charge to watch.
- Rules: projection from the 5th; unusual is 3x the category median of the previous three months with at least 3 records; recurring is the same merchant in both of the last two full months within 10%.
- When the 200-record list is full, the month it only partly covers is ignored.
