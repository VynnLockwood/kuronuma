# Blush look, original motifs and gentle motion

- Agent: claude
- Date: 2026-10-31
- Branch / PR: feat/blush-look, PR opened from this branch

## Goal
Give Kuronuma the Blush palette, six original SVG motifs and gentle motion (calm tone, a little cute), on the landing page, shared components and the Go consent and error pages. The cursor-reactive letter field is a later change (V2).

## What I did
- Theme: Blush tokens in `globals.css`, `themeColor`, and `check:design` now pins background and primary and checks WCAG contrast pairs.
- Motifs: `components/motifs` (sparkle, sprout, cloud, letter, petal, star), placed in empty bands so they never sit over text.
- Motion: hero stagger, twinkle and float, scroll reveal (16 px, 640 ms), `Card cute` lift and sparkle, button shimmer, tab fade, smooth FAQ height, view transition, full reduced-motion fallback.
- Go consent and error pages restyled with colour constants and two inline SVG motifs (no scripts, CSP unchanged).
- New `og.png`, `docs/design.md` Theme and Motion bullets.
- Review fix pass: keyboard focus ring, hover contrast, Thai heading tracking.

## Decisions and assumptions
- Link underline fades by colour instead of sliding in (gradients are banned).
- Sparkle and star sit in a band above the hero; petal and sprout in the product-shot panel's top padding, at every width.
- Hero title is two block spans so each part can rise in separately.
- Tests read `translate` as well as `transform` (Tailwind 4 uses `translate`).
- Amber warning box on the consent page keeps its warning colours.
- Deferred minors (reviewer): `.btn-shimmer` is unlayered; `@view-transition` only affects full page loads, so `docs/design.md` overstates it; `details::details-content` clip applies to every `<details>`; stale `og.png` note under Known gaps and "no motion beyond the tab fade" in the docs; `Card cute` sparkle is the last child; the Go colour test omits the focus outline and amber box (both pass by hand).
