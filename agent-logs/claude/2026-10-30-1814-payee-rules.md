## Decisions and assumptions
- Owner's "approve" on the plan (which asked for approval of migration 00005 and native execution) was taken as both.
- Matching is "contains", so a short payee can match unrelated merchants (`go` matches `Google`); the response always names the rule that matched. An explicit `uncategorized` from the assistant beats a rule (spec).
- Accepted races: `set_payee_rule` racing a category rename or merge can leave a rule pointing at a name that is no longer listed (saves then fall back to `uncategorized`; `list_categories` shows it as `unlisted` and a merge repairs it); the 500 cap can be overshot by the number of calls in flight.
- Open question for the owner: after a merge into `uncategorized`, a rule keeps working as a rule pointing at `uncategorized` and can still beat a shorter rule that points at a real category. I kept "the rule follows its category".
