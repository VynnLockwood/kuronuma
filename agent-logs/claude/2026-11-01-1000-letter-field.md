## Decisions and assumptions
- Opacity 0.18 to 0.28 instead of the spec's 0.35 to 0.7 (AA contrast for muted text and links).
- `stepLetter` allocates a small object per letter per frame (pure API as planned).
- Deferred minors: tilt relaxes to upright; letters do not fill a window resized wider; touch does not clear a stale mouse pointer; letters are hidden on muted sections, the footer and the signed-out dashboard; the Theme bullet's "motifs never over text" needs an exception note; header blur cost on low-end phones; wider browser-check coverage.
